# Devlog - 2026-09-10 (part 4)

## Provenance: `/history`, the Play-and-own Layer Without "Earn"

Grew out of a design conversation about why Play-to-earn blockchain games
collapsed (93% of 2020-2026 launches are effectively dead) and what
"Play-and-own" should actually mean here: transparent, tamper-evident proof
of what you own and how you got it — with no token, no valuation, and no
promise that any of it converts to money.

Turned out most of the hard part already existed. `doc/TRADE.md`'s
`player_trades` ledger has been writing every completed trade — both sides'
gold before/after, every item's `(def, qty, enchant)` — in the *same
transaction* as the trade itself since early on, permanently, with no
delete path. It just had no player-facing query; "raw SQL only" was the
documented answer. `/history` is the read-only layer on top: a player's own
last 20 trades, formatted, with a relative timestamp ("3m ago") instead of a
calendar date (deliberately — correct calendar math needs a date crate this
workspace doesn't have, and getting it silently wrong would undermine a
feature whose whole point is being trustworthy).

New: `server/src/game_state/provenance.rs`, `AuthService::trade_history_for`
+ `TradeHistoryRow` in `auth.rs`, `/history` routed in `chat.rs`. No new
`GameState` field, no new lock, no new writes — it only reads what
`commit_trade` already wrote.

Explicitly scoped out (documented, not forgotten): full single-item
lifetime tracking across owners (the ledger uses `(def, qty, enchant)`, not
`instance_id`, because instance IDs are re-minted per login and would be
meaningless in a permanent record — doc/TRADE.md's existing call); NPC
merchant trades (out of `player_trades`' threat model by design); anything
past a quick personal summary (raw SQL remains the tool for real
investigation).

Full reasoning: `doc/PROVENANCE.md`.
