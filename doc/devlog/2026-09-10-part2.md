# Devlog - 2026-09-10 (part 2)

## Rested Growth, Mindfulness Nudges, Rapport

Three more small, deliberately non-punishing systems, following up on the
non-destructive enchant rework earlier today.

**Rested growth.** Logging back in after 10+ minutes away opens a
time-based (not XP-banked) +50% XP window — one minute of bonus play per
hour offline, capped at two hours. Applied per-recipient in
`grant_monster_kill_xp`, so a party of one rested and one not-rested member
awards each their own amount. Backed by a single new nullable
`characters.logged_out_at` column, stamped by SQLite's own clock on every
save (periodic or logout) rather than anything client-supplied.

**Mindfulness session nudges.** A friendly, judgment-free system message at
roughly 2 and 4 hours into a continuous session — never a countdown, never
a lockout. Session clocks are in-memory only, on a new 60s `"wellbeing"`
background tick.

**Rapport.** The CHA-derived haggle band (`doc/ECONOMY.md`) now also widens
per (player, NPC) pair based on their accepted-deal history — +1 percentage
point of band per 3 accepted deals, capped at +15. Turns what
`doc/ECONOMY.md` had only sketched as LLM persona memory ("Rica remembers
regulars") into something the server actually enforces.

New file: `server/src/game_state/wellbeing.rs`. Full designs in
`doc/REST.md` and the new "단골 보너스" section of `doc/ECONOMY.md`;
implementation status tracked in `doc/NEXT_GEN_VISION.md` section 3.
