# Devlog - 2026-09-10

## Non-Destructive Enchanting

Enchant scrolls no longer destroy the item on a miss. A failed attempt now
fizzles — the weapon or armor piece stays exactly as it was — instead of
bursting into dust the way it did on the old Lineage-style ladder. The
guaranteed zone is also wider (+7 for weapons, +5 for armor, up from +4/+2),
and the odds past it taper more gently to a 20% floor instead of halving
down to 1%.

To keep enchanting a meaningful gold sink without destruction doing the
work, attempts past the guaranteed zone now charge a direct "polishing oil"
fee in copper (0c through +7/+5, scaling up to a flat 50,000c from +12
onward) — the sink `doc/DESIGN_DIRECTION.md` had proposed but never wired
up. Too poor to pay it, and the scroll is kept unread rather than being
spent on a roll you can't afford.

The reasoning: the old destroy-on-fail ladder was a deliberate callback to
Lineage's enchanting, and it worked as a gold sink for exactly the same
reason Lineage's did — by using loss aversion to pull a small number of
heavy players into compulsive, ruinous re-farming loops. Pulling the
destroy step out and replacing it with a priced, bounded, always-affordable
gold sink keeps the risk/reward tension at the top of the ladder without
that mechanism. See `doc/ENCHANT.md` for the full curve and fee table, and
the revision note at the top of the "싱크는 인챈트 도전으로" section in
`doc/DESIGN_DIRECTION.md` for the historical context this replaces.
